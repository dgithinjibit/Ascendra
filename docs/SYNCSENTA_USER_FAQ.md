# Syncsenta User FAQ

**For:** Students, Teachers, Heads of School, and Parents/Guardians  
**About:** Learning activities, AI assistance, feedback, privacy, and offline use

## General questions

### What is Syncsenta?

Syncsenta is a Kenyan CBC learning platform that helps learners practise, receive guidance, and build evidence of progress. Teachers and families can review appropriate progress information, while school leaders can view authorized school-level information.

### Is Syncsenta a replacement for a teacher?

No. Syncsenta provides learning support, practice, explanations, and structured feedback. Teachers remain responsible for professional judgment, assessment decisions, safeguarding, and meaningful learner support. Some questions are deliberately held for teacher, guardian, privacy, or safeguarding review.

### What happens if the AI is unavailable?

The platform uses deterministic local tutoring and cached learning support where possible. Offline or unknown connectivity is routed to the local path rather than repeatedly attempting remote inference. Work can remain queued for synchronization, and objective assessment logic can continue locally when the activity supports it.

### Does Syncsenta work with limited connectivity?

The learning flow is designed to support offline and metered use. Activities and permitted responses can be saved locally, and synchronization can resume later. A delayed synchronization state does not mean that completed work should be repeated automatically.

## Student questions

### How do I start an activity?

Open your assigned grade and subject, select the activity, read the instructions, and submit your answer when ready. If you are offline, continue the activity normally and check the sync status when your connection returns.

### Can I ask for a hint?

Yes. Ask for a hint or explain which step is difficult. The tutor is designed to provide a small next step rather than immediately giving away the answer. You may receive more guided support after repeated attempts or signs of frustration.

### Why did I receive a review message instead of an answer?

Some requests require a consent, safety, privacy, or human-review step. This is intentional. The system should not guess when it lacks the necessary context or when a trusted adult or teacher should be involved.

### How is my progress used?

Progress is used to support learning and authorized feedback. Access is limited by role and relationship. Do not enter passwords, access tokens, or unnecessary personal information into an activity or chat.

## Teacher questions

### How do I give feedback on generated content?

Use the feedback control for the relevant content item. Select a positive or negative rating. If you select a negative rating, describe the specific problem and provide an actionable improvement suggestion, such as a curriculum mismatch, unsuitable difficulty, unclear wording, or missing Kenyan context.

### What content can I review?

You can review learner evidence and assigned-student progress that your authenticated teacher relationship authorizes. You should not see unrelated students or data from another school. Report any unexpected visibility immediately and do not copy the information into email.

### What happens after I submit negative feedback?

The feedback is validated and can be used for quality improvement or routed for human review. The system rejects unsupported content types, oversized fields, and negative feedback without useful context so that the review queue remains actionable.

### How do I escalate a learner concern?

Use the appropriate review category: learning support, safeguarding, well-being, content correction, or privacy. Include only the minimum necessary context. Immediate safety concerns must follow the school’s safeguarding process and must not rely on the AI assistant alone.

## Head of School questions

### What can I see?

A Head of School can review authorized school-level progress and operational information for the relevant school. Access is not a license to view every learner record across schools. Cross-school visibility, unexpected identifiers, or a suspected policy bypass must be reported as a privacy incident.

### Can I use Syncsenta scores as the only basis for a decision?

No. Learning evidence and scores are inputs for professional review, not an automatic disciplinary, placement, health, or safeguarding determination. Interpret results together with teacher observations and the learner’s context.

### How do I report a platform or privacy issue?

Record the visible error, route, approximate time, deployment or activity version if shown, and an opaque case identifier. Do not include passwords, tokens, raw prompts, full learner conversations, or unnecessary identifiers.

## Parent/Guardian questions

### What information can I receive about my child?

A verified parent or guardian can receive authorized information about linked-child progress, consent state, and appropriate teacher feedback. The child relationship must be confirmed before information is disclosed.

### Why can’t I see my child’s progress yet?

The account may not be authenticated as the intended guardian, the child relationship may be missing or incomplete, or the information may require privacy review. Do not send identity documents or sensitive learner details through ordinary email unless the school’s approved process specifically requests them.

### Can I manage consent?

Where enabled for your account, you can review or manage consent for the linked child. A denied, unknown, expired, or guardian-required consent state may hold some AI interactions until the appropriate review is complete.

### Will I receive every learner conversation by email?

No. Email is not an approved channel for raw learner conversations, prompts, telemetry, passwords, tokens, or safeguarding details. Authorized dashboards and approved school processes should be used instead.

## Privacy, safety, and account questions

### Does Syncsenta send my private learning data by email?

The application is designed not to send raw learner telemetry or conversations by email. Operational notifications should contain only minimal status information. If an email appears to include private learner data, do not forward it; report it through the school’s approved privacy channel.

### What should I do if I see the wrong learner’s information?

Stop viewing the record, do not copy or share it, note the time and visible route, and report the incident to the school or privacy contact. Do not attempt to fix the issue by changing relationships or disabling security controls.

### What should I do in an immediate safety emergency?

Contact a trusted adult, the school’s safeguarding lead, or the appropriate local emergency service. Syncsenta can route a review request, but it is not an emergency service and must not be the only response to immediate danger.

### How do I report a bug?

Include the role you were using, device/browser, connectivity state, activity or route, approximate time, visible error, and opaque request or queue ID if available. Exclude passwords, tokens, full learner identifiers, raw conversations, and unnecessary personal data.

## Quick support table

| Problem | First step | Escalate to |
|---|---|---|
| Activity will not submit | Check local save and retry after reconnection | Teacher or technical support |
| Feedback seems wrong | Record activity/content ID and submit actionable correction feedback | Teacher/content reviewer |
| Child cannot be found | Verify the authenticated account and relationship | Account/privacy support |
| Safety concern | Stop normal troubleshooting and use safeguarding procedure | Safeguarding lead |
| Unexpected learner visibility | Stop viewing and preserve only minimal evidence | Privacy/security lead |
| Slow or unavailable AI | Use offline/cache path and record the visible status | Technical support |
