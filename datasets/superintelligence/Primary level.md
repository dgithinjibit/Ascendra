# PRIMARY SCHOOL (Grades 4–6, Ages 9–11)
## AI Discovery Level

### Grade 4: "What is AI?"
- **Module 1:** Technology in our world – computers, smartphones, tablets.
- **Module 2:** What is Artificial Intelligence? – stories, examples, games.
- **Module 3:** AI that creates – fun drawing and storytelling tools (simple interfaces).
- **Module 4:** Responsible use – online safety, creating good content.

**Resources:** Use kid‑friendly AI tools (e.g., QuickDraw, AutoDraw, simple Gemini chat) – always supervised.

---

### Grade 5: "Talking to AI"
- **Module 1:** Giving clear instructions – programming your friend (unplugged).
- **Module 2:** Asking good questions to get good answers.
- **Module 3:** AI in Kenya – examples in agriculture, health, education.
- **Module 4:** AI art and stories – create Kenyan folktales with AI.

**Resources:** Google Colab with simple prompts; Gemini API playground (under supervision).

---

### Grade 6: "AI Projects"
- **Module 1:** AI for nature and wildlife – conservation, Kenyan animals.
- **Module 2:** AI for health – how computers help doctors.
- **Module 3:** Responsible AI – ethics, avoiding harm.
- **Module 4:** Capstone – identify a community problem and propose an AI solution (presentation).

**Resources:** Cloud Skills Boost introductory labs (with help).

---

# JUNIOR SECONDARY SCHOOL (Grades 7–9, Ages 12–15)
## AI Explorer Level

**See Document 1 for a full weekly syllabus.** Below is the curriculum structure with direct links to all labs/notebooks.

### Grade 7: AI Foundations
- Generative AI concepts, history, types.
- **Labs:** [Getting Started with Vertex AI Gemini API](https://www.cloudskillsboost.google/catalog_lab/1281) and [Multimodality with Gemini](https://www.cloudskillsboost.google/catalog_lab/1281).
- Creative AI – art, music, writing.
- Using AI as a study partner.

### Grade 8: AI Skills Development
- Prompt engineering – [Intro Gemini](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/generative-ai/blob/main/gemini/intro_gemini.ipynb), [Chain of Thought & ReAct](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/generative-ai/blob/main/gemini/chain_of_thought_react.ipynb), [Safety Ratings](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/generative-ai/blob/main/gemini/safety_ratings_thresholds.ipynb).
- Code generation – [Code Generation Notebook](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/generative-ai/blob/main/code_generation/code_generation.ipynb).
- Building simple chatbots – [Create a Generative Chat App Codelab](https://codelabs.developers.google.com/codelabs/vertex-ai-conversation-chat).
- Ethics – bias, privacy, fairness.

### Grade 9: AI Project Development
- Advanced concepts – embeddings, vector search ([Notebook](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/generative-ai/blob/main/embeddings/text_embeddings_vector_search.ipynb)), RAG ([Multimodal RAG Notebook](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/generative-ai/blob/main/rag/multimodal_rag_gemini.ipynb)).
- Business applications in Kenya (agritech, fintech, tourism).
- Design and present an AI solution for a local problem (capstone).

# SENIOR SECONDARY SCHOOL (Grades 10–12, Ages 16–18)
## AI Developer Level

### Grade 10: AI Engineering
- Deep learning fundamentals – neural networks, training, evaluation.
- Natural Language Processing – embeddings, language models.
- Computer Vision – image processing, object detection.
- **Notebooks:** [Text Embeddings](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/generative-ai/blob/main/embeddings/text_embeddings_vector_search.ipynb), [Image Generation with Imagen](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/generative-ai/blob/main/imagen/create_high_quality_visual_assets.ipynb).

### Grade 11: AI Systems
- Large Language Models (LLMs) – transformers, fine‑tuning.
- Building AI agents and assistants.
- Responsible AI – explainability, safety, regulation.
- MLOps – model lifecycle, deployment, monitoring.
- **Notebooks:** [Tuning a foundation model](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/generative-ai/blob/main/tuning/tuning_foundation_model.ipynb), [RLHF](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/generative-ai/blob/main/rlhf/rlhf_vertex_ai.ipynb), [AutoSxS Model Evaluation](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/generative-ai/blob/main/evaluation/autosxs_model_eval.ipynb).

### Grade 12: AI Innovation
- Emerging technologies – multimodal AI, robotics.
- Reading research papers – methodology and trends.
- AI entrepreneurship – business models, pitching.
- **Capstone:** Full AI project from concept to deployment, with documentation and presentation.

## Implementation for Kenyan Schools & Homeschools

**For Parents/Guardians (Homeschool):**
- Create a dedicated learning space with reliable internet.
- Supervise all cloud activities; set up accounts together.
- Monitor free credit usage ($300).
- Encourage collaboration with other homeschoolers.

**For Schools:**
- Ensure devices and internet connectivity.
- Train teachers (Google Cloud certification recommended).
- Partner with Google Education and local tech companies.
- Roll out in phases (pilot → gradual → full).
